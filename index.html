Before you begin
This rehabilitation programme has been designed by Chiropractic by G to support your recovery following assessment by one of our practitioners.
Please read before starting:
  • Perform each programme only if it has been recommended specifically for you.
  • Complete each exercise slowly and with good control.
  • Mild discomfort or muscle fatigue is normal, but stop immediately if you experience severe pain, dizziness, numbness, weakness, loss of balance or any other concerning symptoms.
  • Never force a movement beyond what feels comfortable.
  • Progress through the programme only when instructed within the app.
  • If your symptoms worsen, fail to improve, or you develop new symptoms, discontinue the exercises and contact Chiropractic by G or your healthcare provider.
